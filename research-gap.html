Verified gap

The closest published Hamming (11,7) FS-GDI study does not provide an extended-parity encoder, machine-readable experiment manifest, complete reproducible netlists, or a matched multi-condition comparison of CMOS, FS-GDI, and selective restoration on the deeper P0 path.

Proposed response

The project introduces an extended Hamming (12,7) evaluation package and a limited Hybrid-B architecture: FS-GDI forms the parity networks while CMOS restores only externally observed parity outputs. All architectures use the same predictive model, stimuli, measurement definitions, and parameter sweeps.

Contribution supported by current evidence

  • A reproducible LTspice corpus of 121 experiments with validation status and provenance.
  • Exhaustive software verification of 128 messages, all 1,536 single-bit errors, and all 8,448 double-bit errors.
  • A speed-oriented hybrid that improves nominal power, delay, PDP, and transistor count relative to the CMOS reference.
  • An explicit negative result: unbuffered FS-GDI remains substantially lower in power and PDP than the hybrid.

Novelty boundary: this is a defensible candidate contribution, not a claim of universal novelty or publication. A broader systematic search and scholarly review are still required before formal novelty claims.
