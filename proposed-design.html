Codeword and equations

Data D1–D7 occupy positions 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, and 11. Even-parity bits occupy positions 1, 2, 4, and 8; P0 is the overall parity bit at position 12.

P1 = D1 ⊕ D2 ⊕ D4 ⊕ D5 ⊕ D7   (1)
P2 = D1 ⊕ D3 ⊕ D4 ⊕ D6 ⊕ D7   (2)
P4 = D2 ⊕ D3 ⊕ D4;   P8 = D5 ⊕ D6 ⊕ D7   (3)
P0 = D1 ⊕ ··· ⊕ D7 ⊕ P1 ⊕ P2 ⊕ P4 ⊕ P8   (4)

Architecture

CMOS supplies the full-swing reference. FS-GDI reduces device count and switched capacitance. Hybrid-A buffers every XOR output and serves as an intentionally heavy restoration reference. Proposed Hybrid-B applies CMOS buffers only to the five externally observed parity outputs, using 152 transistors versus 264 for CMOS and 132 for FS-GDI.

Design rationale and trade-offs

Selective restoration targets external drive and the measured P0 path without paying the capacitance cost at every internal node. The design improves nominal critical-path speed relative to both CMOS and FS-GDI, but its restoration energy prevents it from matching the minimum-power FS-GDI result.

Verification

Boolean functions are verified exhaustively in Python and represented independently in SystemVerilog. Transistor-level experiments apply a common 16-cycle vector sequence, predictive model, device sizing, supply, load, and measurement definition. This study does not include a transistor-level decoder or post-layout extraction.
