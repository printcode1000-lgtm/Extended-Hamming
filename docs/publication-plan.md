# Publication Plan

## Candidate theme

Selective CMOS Restoration in an FS-GDI Extended Hamming (12,7) Encoder: A Reproducible 65-nm Predictive LTspice Study

## Evidence to use

1. Independent reproduction of the Hamming (11,7) parity network.
2. Exhaustive SEC-DED functional verification.
3. CMOS, FS-GDI, Hybrid-A, and Hybrid-B transistor-level netlists.
4. Nominal D1-to-P0 power, delay, PDP, energy, swing, and transistor counts.
5. One-factor robustness sweeps and transparent rejected points.

## Publication claim boundary

The paper may describe Hybrid-B as a speed-oriented compromise only. It must also report that FS-GDI has lower simulated power and PDP. Direct comparison with papers using a foundry PDK, different VDD, different loading, or post-layout extraction must be qualified.

## Remaining work before submission

- Supervisor review of the reconstructed six-transistor FS-GDI topology.
- Optional independent HSPICE/Virtuoso cross-check.
- A broader systematic search in subscription databases.
- Language and similarity review under the university's rules.
