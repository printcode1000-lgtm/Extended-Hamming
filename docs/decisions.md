# Research Decisions

1. Extended Hamming is treated as established theory, not novelty.
2. Hybrid-A restores every XOR output; Hybrid-B restores only encoder parity outputs.
3. The final proposed candidate is Hybrid-B because it offers the best measured D1-to-P0 delay among the evaluated encoders, while its energy disadvantage relative to FS-GDI is retained.
4. P0, not P1, is the extended-encoder delay endpoint. All EH127 experiments were rerun after this audit correction.
5. No layout-area claim or fabricated process corner is allowed.
6. Three overshoot cases are rejected rather than edited or omitted.
